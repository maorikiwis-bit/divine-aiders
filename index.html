Responsible borrowing

Understand your repayment obligations

A loan is a commitment. Before you borrow, make sure you understand:

  • The total amount you will repay, including all fees and interest.
  • Your repayment dates and what happens if a payment is missed.
  • Whether the repayments fit comfortably within your monthly budget.

Only borrow what you need and can afford to repay. If anything is unclear, ask us before signing.
